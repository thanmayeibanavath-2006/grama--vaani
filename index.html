About Our Project

Bridging the digital divide in rural governance

Project Overview

Grama Vaani aims to address the communication gap between Gram Panchayats and villagers by providing a digital platform for grievance submission, tracking, and resolution. The goal is to support transparent governance and timely responses to community needs.

Key Features

  • Online grievance submission
  • Grievance reference number and status lookup
  • Panchayat-facing dashboard concept
  • Feedback collection
  • Responsive mobile-friendly layout
